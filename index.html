What we are asking for

🥗
DEMAND 01

At least 2 affordable healthy options

Every canteen should serve at least two affordable, healthy food options, every day, alongside everything else on the menu.

🧼
DEMAND 02

Clean and hygienic food

Food should be cooked, stored and served in clean, hygienic conditions, with clean utensils and safe drinking water.

📋
DEMAND 03

Calories and nutrition on the menu

Canteen menus should show the calories and nutritional values of every item, so students can make informed choices.
