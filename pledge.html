Run a canteen? Pledge to improve it.

Any school, college, office or hospital can commit to serving food that is hygienic, affordable and healthy. Tell us what you will work on, and we will publish your name alongside the institutions leading the way.

🏢 This is an institutional pledge, not an anonymous signature. We ask for a contact person so we can verify it.

The pledge

🥗
COMMITMENT 01

At least 2 affordable healthy options

Serve at least two affordable, healthy options every day, alongside everything else on the menu.

🧼
COMMITMENT 02

Clean and hygienic food

Cook, store and serve food in clean conditions, with clean utensils and safe drinking water.

📋
COMMITMENT 03

Calories and nutrition on the menu

Show calories and nutritional values so people can make informed choices.
